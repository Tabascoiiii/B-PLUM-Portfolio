English

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Desktop Time Companion does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Timer state, app settings, and theme selection remain locally on the user's Mac.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The app reads StoreKit-supplied, verified transaction entitlements to unlock purchased themes. It does not receive payment-card information or Apple Account credentials.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, account system, or third-party data collection service.
